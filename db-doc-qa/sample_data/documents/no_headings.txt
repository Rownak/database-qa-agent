Our warehouse team checks every outgoing parcel before it leaves the building. Each parcel is weighed, labelled and scanned, and the scan is recorded in the tracking system so that the customer can follow the shipment from the moment it is collected.

When a parcel arrives late, the team looks at the tracking history first. Most late parcels are caused by missed connections at a sorting hub, and these usually recover within a day or two without any action from us.

If a parcel has not moved for several days, the team contacts the carrier and asks for a recovery plan. The customer is kept informed with short, factual updates until the parcel is delivered or replaced.

Damaged parcels are photographed on arrival and the photos are attached to the shipment record. This makes later claims faster, because the evidence is already in place when the customer asks for compensation.
