# Parcel Handling Guide

Internal notes on how parcels are handled at the depot. This file is a Markdown test fixture.

## 1. Receiving

### 1.1 Inbound scan
Every inbound parcel is scanned at the dock door. The scan creates a tracking event with the time and the dock number.

### 1.2 Damage check
(a) Photograph any parcel with visible damage before it moves.
(b) Attach the photos to the shipment record.
(c) Tell the shift supervisor if more than three parcels in one delivery are damaged.

## 2. Dispatch

### 2.1 Loading
Parcels are loaded by delivery route. Heavy items go on the bottom of the cage and fragile items on top.

### 2.2 Missed pickups
If a carrier misses a pickup, staff must:

- record the missed pickup in the tracking system;
- call the carrier to agree a new pickup time;
- notify the customer if the delay is longer than one day.

## 3. Closing the Day
Reconcile the day's scans against the dispatch list and report any parcel that was scanned in but not dispatched.
